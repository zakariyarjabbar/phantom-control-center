# PHANTOM — Control Center

## Platform

Web, desktop and mobile. Fictional entertainment and exploration software.

## Users and job

Visitors discover a signal, decode coordinates, reveal a station, trace its network, and unlock authored archives. Operate directly, without a marketing page. All operational content is simulated.

## Stack

User delegated the modern stack choice. React 19, TypeScript, Next.js for Vercel, local browser persistence, no paid APIs or live operational systems. The original Vinext/Sites scripts remain optional legacy tooling; Vercel is the requested deployment target.

## Confirmed requirements

Green world map and globe; station inspector; terminal; tracing; constellation; signal lab; archives; four puzzle types; packet stream; deterministic replay timeline; three scenarios and free exploration; window management and saved workspaces; actionable events; derived telemetry; verified external directory; startup; optional audio; secrets; settings.

## Brand commitments

Nearly black, forest-green surfaces, phosphor highlights, technical thin borders, restrained CRT texture and motion. Dense yet readable specialist software. Map dominates first viewport. Compact status bar, navigation rail, right inspector, lower terminal/feed and panel dock.

## Success

Every visible control acts; panels share identities and simulation clock. Approximately 16 stations and 24 authored archive files. Discoveries and preferences survive reloads; replay preserves unlocked discoveries. Keyboard, responsive and reduced-motion support.
